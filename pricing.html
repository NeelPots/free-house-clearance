Common Questions

Before You Decide

What happens if I stop paying the monthly fee?

Your website stays live exactly as it is — you just won't get further edits or changes included until you resume, or you can move it elsewhere at any time.

Is there a contract or tie-in period?

No. Both plans are pay-monthly with no fixed term — cancel whenever you like.

What counts as an "edit"?

Text changes, new photos, updated prices or hours, new sections, small design tweaks — the day-to-day updates most local business sites need.

How does this compare to what I pay now?

Send over your current provider's invoice/contract and we'll put together a direct side-by-side comparison so you can see exactly where you'd save.
